# Team handoff

Use this specialist to assess significance, not to approve adoption. Preferred finding: breakthrough with qualified Promethean potential. Strongest counterargument: projected breadth and task assistance do not establish durable civilizational transformation; reliable deployment and complements remain unresolved.

Integration rule: broad significance may increase monitoring priority or learning value, but adoption requires separate application value, readiness, consequence assessment and accountable human judgment. Keep general classification stable for the same evidence. Pass uncertainty, source dates, dissent and abstention to the orchestrator; do not collapse these into a single enthusiasm score.

Open question: what observed cross-sector institutional change should count as sufficient evidence to move from potential to a stronger claim? Calibrate against Chapter 11 and the lecture before treating these operational criteria as course-authoritative.
