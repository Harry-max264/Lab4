# Verified source register

Verification date: 2026-10-19. Fixed evidence snapshot, not a comprehensive October 2026 market survey.

## S1
Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, Generative AI at Work, https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11771v2

2024-11-06 revision; abstract inspected 2026-10-19. A field study reports heterogeneous customer-support productivity gains with AI assistance. One deployment, not universal ROI; used qualitatively because earlier and revised versions differ.

## S2
Eloundou et al., GPTs are GPTs, https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10130v5

2023-08-21 revision; abstract inspected 2026-10-19. Task-exposure analysis indicates a pathway to effects across occupations and a role for complementary software. Exposure is not realized adoption or durable societal transformation; authors do not forecast adoption timing.

## S3
NIST AI 600-1, https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf

2024-07-26; report text inspected 2026-10-19. Generative AI can produce erroneous content and create overreliance and privacy risks. Sections 2.2, 2.4 and 2.7; risk taxonomy, not evidence of clinical efficacy or binding approval.
