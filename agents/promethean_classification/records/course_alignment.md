The chapter treats generative AI as Potential Promethean. This agent adopts that prospective calibration with qualifications, while recording that its small independent evidence set does not itself establish a tenfold effect, mature irreversibility or confirmed multi-tier dissolution. This disagreement in evidence strength is retained rather than rounded up. The chapter is a working framework offered by its author, not a consensus empirical finding.

Scoring uses transparent evidence grades because the chapter gives criteria but no numeric weighting or acceptance cutoff. A failed or unresolved gate cannot be hidden by a total score. Exact lecture-specific additions and Brightspace submission settings were not available for verification.
